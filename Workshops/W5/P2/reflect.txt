In C++, the friend keyword allows a function or another class to access private and protected 
members of the class in which it is declared. 
comparison operators (operator< and operator>) and I/O operators (operator<< and operator>>) are declared as friends of 
the Hero class. This design choice allows these operators to access private data members (name, powers, numPowers, powerLevel) 
directly, which is necessary for their operations.
For example, operator< and operator> need to compare 
powerLevel of two Hero objects to determine their ordering. 
Since powerLevel is a private member, these operators would not have access to it without being declared as friends.
